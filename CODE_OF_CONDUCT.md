# Code of Conduct

Worship Presentation Assistant is built for worship teams and communities. Everyone participating in this project deserves a respectful environment, regardless of experience, language, disability, identity, background, religious belief or lack of belief.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient and constructive, especially with people learning the application or contributing for the first time.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking the people behind them.
- Respect different churches, traditions and beliefs. Keep project discussions focused on the software and the practical needs of its users.
- Give useful feedback, acknowledge mistakes and respect another person's boundaries.
- Protect people's privacy and share only content you are entitled to publish.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory remarks or personal attacks.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual content or attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated unwanted contact, targeted disruption, spam or deliberate derailment of discussions.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or participating in a review.

## Scope

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces. They also apply when someone explicitly represents the project in another public setting. Disagreement about a contribution or belief does not excuse abusive behavior.

## Reporting a concern

Send concerns privately to [melih_dogann@outlook.com](mailto:melih_dogann@outlook.com) with the subject `WPA community concern`. Include relevant links, dates, a description of what happened and any immediate safety or privacy concerns. Do not publish another person's private information in an issue to make a report.

If the concern involves someone participating in moderation, say so in your report so that person can step back from handling it where possible. Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is needed to assess and address the concern; complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed.

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask for a change in behavior, edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Decisions should consider the severity, context and pattern of behavior and be applied consistently.

An affected participant may request reconsideration through the reporting address and provide additional context. Reports and appeals are handled as maintainer availability permits.
